:::note[RoboStack is now a monorepo]
Humble, Jazzy, Lyrical and Rolling are now built from one repository, [RoboStack/robostack](https://github.com/RoboStack/robostack).
[Read what changed](/Monorepo.html).
:::

We strive to make our documentation as clear as possible, but sometimes things can get confusing. Good news – our documentation is open-source! You can view and contribute to it on [github](https://github.com/RoboStack/robostack.github.io).

How to help improve our documentation:

- For typos, grammar, or other errors, we'd appreciate your support! Simply [fork](https://github.com/RoboStack/robostack.github.io/fork) our repo, make the necessary changes, and submit a pull request.
- If you have questions or need help, feel free to create an [issue](https://github.com/RoboStack/robostack.github.io/issues) or join the community in the [robotics channel on the prefix.dev Discord](https://discord.gg/kKV8ZxyzY4).
- Prefer talking to us live? Join the monthly [community meeting](/CommunityMeeting.html).

We're always eager to improve, and your input is valuable to us. Thank you for being part of the RoboStack community!

## Experiencing ROS Distro-Specific Issues?

If you're facing any issues specific to a ROS distribution, we recommend creating an issue in the corresponding GitHub repository.
You can also contribute by following the guidelines in our [contribution](/Contributing.html) guide.

- [ROS2 Humble, Jazzy, Lyrical and Rolling](https://github.com/RoboStack/robostack/issues/new/choose): these are all built from the [robostack](https://github.com/RoboStack/robostack) repository, so mention the distribution in your issue.

The following distributions are deprecated and not actively maintained anymore. Their repositories are kept around, but expect little to no response on new issues:

- [ROS Noetic](https://github.com/RoboStack/ros-noetic/issues/new/choose) (deprecated)
- [ROS2 Kilted](https://github.com/RoboStack/ros-kilted/issues/new/choose) (deprecated)
- [ROS2 Galactic](https://github.com/RoboStack/ros-galactic/issues/new/choose) (deprecated)
- [ROS2 Foxy](https://github.com/RoboStack/ros-foxy/issues/new/choose) (deprecated)

By submitting your issue to the appropriate repo, we can better understand and address your concerns. Thank you for helping us improve the RoboStack ecosystem!
